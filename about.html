About Me

Hello!! I’m Lineth Garach, an actor, dancer, and overall performer. Performing has been a part of my life for as long as I can remember, and it has played a huge role in shaping the person I am today. I began ballet at just three years old, and from there, my love for performing and being on stage has only continued to grow.

From Ballet to the Stage

I danced ballet from ages three to thirteen, spending much of that time training with Princeton Ballet School and American Repertory Ballet. During my ballet training, I was a Dance Power Scholarship recipient at Princeton Ballet School and also trained en pointe.

After ballet, I continued exploring movement through different styles of dance, including jazz, lyrical, and hip-hop. My love for performing eventually expanded beyond dance and into acting, musical theatre, and voice. I also have three years of choir experience and sing in the alto/mezzo-soprano range.

Each new form of performance has given me another way to tell a story. As I continue growing as a performer, I’ve also become increasingly interested in what happens beyond the spotlight. Directing has given me the opportunity to explore the creative world behind the stage, and I hope to continue discovering both sides of theatre in the years ahead.

Continuing My Artistic Education

I am currently studying at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), where I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Engineering Technology with a minor in Theater Arts and Technology. My expected graduation is May 2029. While my education combines two very different fields, the performing arts continue to be an important part of my college experience and my growth as an artist.

My artistic interests include acting, dance, musical theatre, directing, and, most importantly, finding new ways to bring stories to life.

What I’m Working On

I am currently performing as both Fanny Dashwood and Lady Middleton in Sense & Sensibility, directed by Rodney Reyes at the Jim Wise Theater at NJIT. Performances are November 5, 6, and 7, 2026. Taking on two different characters in the same production has given me the opportunity to challenge myself as an actor and explore two very different personalities onstage.

At the same time, I am stepping behind the scenes to direct Island Time, a musical written by Danielle Koenig. Performances are December 4 and 5, 2026 at 7:00 PM in the Jim Wise Theater at NJIT. Directing this production is allowing me to explore another side of theatre and continue expanding the kind of artist I want to become.
