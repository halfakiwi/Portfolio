About Me

Hi, I’m Lineth Garach, an actor, dancer, and performer. Performing has been an important part of my life since I began ballet at age three. My interests have since grown to include acting, dance, musical theatre, directing, and storytelling through performance.

From Ballet to the Stage

I studied ballet from age three to thirteen, building ten years of training that included Princeton Ballet School / American Repertory Ballet. I was a Dance Power Scholarship recipient, and I am pointe trained.

Alongside ballet, my dance background includes three years of training and experience in jazz, lyrical, and hip-hop. I also have three years of choir experience, with an alto / mezzo vocal range. I am fluent in English and Spanish.

These different forms of expression are part of my continuing growth as a performer, from movement and voice to acting and directing.

Continuing My Artistic Education

I am currently studying at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), where I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Engineering Technology with a minor in Theater Arts and Technology. My expected graduation is May 2029, and my artistic interests remain centered on acting, dance, musical theatre, directing, and storytelling.

What I’m Working On

I am currently performing as both Fanny Dashwood and Lady Middleton in Sense & Sensibility, directed by Rodney Reyes at the Jim Wise Theater at NJIT. Performances are November 5, 6, and 7, 2026.

I am also directing Island Time, a musical written by Danielle Koenig. Performances are December 4 and 5, 2026 at 7:00 PM.
